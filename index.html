The Most Unwritten Automation

A private production tool that helps the creator of the YouTube channel The Most Unwritten write, narrate, quality-check, upload and schedule its animated Roblox story Shorts.

The Most Unwritten is a first-person mystery series told with classic Roblox characters. The Automation runs on the creator's own Windows computer and connects only to the creator's own YouTube channel. It is not a public service: no one else can sign in to it, and it does not access anyone else's account.

Writes and narrates

Generates each new episode's script, storyboard, title and description from the series' own story notes, and narrates it with the series' approved AI voice.

Checks quality

Reviews every finished video for continuity, animation, caption and pacing problems before it can be published.

Uploads and schedules

Uploads approved episodes to the creator's channel as private videos scheduled for 4:00 PM Eastern, one per day, and confirms the schedule with YouTube.

Learns from results

Reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, retention) so future episodes can improve.

How it uses YouTube

The Automation uses YouTube API Services with permission the channel owner grants through Google's sign-in. It uploads videos, sets their publication schedule, reads the channel's video list to avoid duplicate uploads, and reads the channel's analytics. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Full details of what data is accessed, where it is stored, who it is shared with and how to revoke access are in our Privacy Policy.
